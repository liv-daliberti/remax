# E135 preregistration: RLEP-Dr with an online experience pool

Date frozen: 2026-09-24, before submission of any E135 cell.

## The question this answers

Table 1 places RLEP-Dr, which also rehearses verified successes, at roughly
zero PCMD beside Re:Dr's +.29. App. "What RLEP-Dr replays" explains the gap
from the pools themselves: RLEP's protocol harvests its pool from a policy RL
has already trained, here the paired terminal Dr.GRPO control, and at
Qwen2.5-0.5B all but 17 of 4,393 replay-eligible prompts hold a single
canonical mode, so a two-row replay draw repeats one mode with probability
at least .99. That establishes that the offline pool *cannot* carry breadth.
It does not by itself establish that the update rule is irrelevant.

The two updates differ in form. RLEP's replay row enters the group with
advantage $1-\bar r$ under a common baseline, a coefficient that shrinks to
zero as the prompt is solved; Re:Dr's replay term is a fixed-coefficient
length-normalized likelihood on one exemplar per mode. E130 already shows that
the fixed-coefficient term on a single online success recovers 57% of Re:Dr's
PCMD effect. E135 asks the converse: does RLEP's own update recover breadth
once its pool is collected online, from the learner's own rollouts, at the
time of discovery?

## What moves

Relative to the E128 control objective, four keys move: the variant becomes
`rlep`, the replay dose is 2 rows, `OAT_ZERO_RLEP_ONLINE_POOL=1`, and
`OAT_ZERO_RLEP_SPARSE_FALLBACK=1`. The launcher's drift guard refuses a fifth.

Relative to the offline RLEP-Dr cells (E98-R1, E116), exactly one thing moves:
the pool. Held from those cells:

- 16 fresh rows plus 2 replayed successes under one common baseline; fresh
  advantages scaled by 16/18, replay-row advantage $1-\bar r_{\text{mixed}}$;
- replay rows are decoded text, re-tokenized, scored with the Dr.GRPO length
  constant, sampled two without replacement with frequency preserved;
- eligibility requires at least two stored verified trajectories on the prompt;
  an ineligible prompt takes the unchanged 16-row Dr.GRPO update;
- the inert canonical-replay traversal and verified-discovery tracking are
  off, as the `rlep` variant branch has always set them.

The pool starts empty. After every optimizer update, each validator-positive
fresh response of the group is appended to its prompt's list. The replay draw
for a group is taken *before* that group's rows are committed, so a replayed
success is always a past success. The pool is checkpointed with the learner
state and restored on resume; a resume without pool state fails closed.

## Registered predictions

Let $\Delta$ denote the paired five-domain mean terminal PCMD effect against
the E128 control, and let $\Delta_{\text{Re:Dr}} = +.368$ be E132's.

1. **Breadth appears.** $\Delta_{\text{E135}} > 0$ on the five-domain mean,
   and positive in a majority of the 25 paired cells. The offline RLEP-Dr
   arm is at zero by construction; if online collection alone leaves the
   arm there, the update rule is what separates the families.
2. **Recovered fraction.** $\Delta_{\text{E135}} / \Delta_{\text{Re:Dr}}$ is
   registered to fall in $[.25, .75]$. Below .25 reads as "the loss form
   matters": a self-extinguishing advantage row rehearses too weakly once the
   prompt is solved. Above .75 reads as "the pool is essentially the whole
   story" and the comparator row in Table 1 is a statement about RLEP's
   collection protocol rather than about replay through the advantage.
3. **Correctness.** Terminal `pass@8` exceeds the E128 control on the
   five-domain mean. RLEP-Dr already improves correctness where its pool is
   populated; an online pool is populated on more prompts.
4. **Realized dose.** The replay-update fraction exceeds the offline arm's
   (21%--57% at 0.5B) in every domain from the second pass onward, and the
   mean replay advantage on eligible updates is reported beside it.

Predictions 1 and 2 are the ones that change what the paper says. Whichever
way 2 falls, the sentence "what separates the two replay families is the
buffer, not the loss" is rewritten from the measurement.

- Domains: Graph Coloring, Countdown, PythonFactors, MathIR, PantryPlan.
- Seeds: 43--47 (25 scientific cells).

  rate 2e-7, beta=0, one PPO epoch, E78 decoding and evaluation schedule.
- Runtime: the E126--E133 base with the declared source change below; the
  snapshot audit requires the observed divergence to equal the declared set.
- Placement: `cs` partition, `allcs` account, A5000, node203 or node204, as
  E128/E130/E132/E133.
- Control: the completed E128 cells, paired within domain and seed.
  Treatment reference: E132. Offline sibling: E98-R1/E116.

## Declared source change

`src/oat_drgrpo/rlep.py` gains `OnlineRLEPExperiencePool`; `args.py` the
`rlep_online_pool` flag and its validation; `learner/grpo.py` builds that pool
when the flag is set and commits the group's verified rows after the replay
draw; `learner/run.py` checkpoints and restores the pool; `ops/train.sh` and
`ops/run_experiment.sh` pass the flag through. Every hunk is unreachable
unless `rlep_online_pool` is set, so the snapshot is runtime-identical to
E132's for every other arm.

## Addendum, 2026-09-26: PantryPlan resubmission

Twenty cells (Graph, Countdown, Python, MathIR) completed at 3,072 updates and
are unchanged by this addendum. All five PantryPlan cells failed at their
first update with `RuntimeError: RLEP online pool stores decoded text and does
not support canonical action rows`; the watchdog read the crash as a stall
and requeued each cell hourly until the jobs (31510827--31510831) were
cancelled after their fourth restart. Their step-0 attempts were moved aside
under a `.crashed_20260926` suffix and are not evidence.

**Cause.** The pool stored a verified fresh row as the tokenizer's decoded
text. PantryPlan rows are canonical action rows (one registered placeholder
token per decision), whose decoded text is not the response the grader saw,
and the learner refused them rather than store a row it could not replay.

**Source change, declared.** `src/oat_drgrpo/canonical_actions.py` gains
`canonical_action_code_from_token_ids`, the inverse of the existing
`canonical_action_code_token_ids`. `learner/grpo.py` projects a canonical
fresh row to its benchmark response (`decode_canonical_action_response`)
before the pool stores it, and drops the refusal. This is the text the
offline pool holds for these prompts (harvested from the grader-facing
responses) and the text the existing replay draw already projects back onto
the action surface, so the update on PantryPlan is RLEP-Dr's, as registered.
The hunk is unreachable unless `rlep_online_pool` is set on a canonical task.
Covered by `tests/test_pantry_support_mask.py::test_online_rlep_pool_stores_pantry_rows_as_benchmark_responses`.

**Resubmission.** The five PantryPlan cells only, same arm, objective, seeds
and placement, behind a PantryPlan s43 smoke of two passes over 32 prompts so
the second pass exercises the repaired path
(`ops/exp_scaling/launch_e135_pantry_resubmit_05b.py`, ledger
`var/artifacts/e135_online_rlep_05b_pantry_resubmit_jobs.json`). The runtime
is the repaired patch set (base plus the E126--E133 set, `rlep.py` and
`canonical_actions.py`); the control branch of `run_experiment.sh` in that set
passes the entropy coefficient through for E134, which does not reach the
`rlep` variant. The registered predictions are unchanged and are scored on
all 25 cells once PantryPlan lands; the four-domain reading available before
then is reported as such.
